# ScreenTime

ScreenTime helps private groups of friends plan movie nights together.

## Language

**Group**:
A private community of participants who plan movie nights together.

**Participant**:
A person represented in a group's movie-night activity, whether or not they have an account.
_Avoid_: Guest account, temporary account (for a participant without an account)

**Member**:
A person whose account has approved access to a group.
_Avoid_: Participant (when specifically referring to account access)

**Participant without an account**:
A participant whose involvement is recorded by group managers or event editors rather than through their own account access.
_Avoid_: Pending member, guest account

**Account link**:
The confirmed association between an account and an existing participant in a particular group.

**Sign-in method linking**:
The association of multiple sign-in methods with one ScreenTime account, distinct from an account link to a group participant.

**Membership request**:
A request for an account to receive access to a private group.

**Group owner**:
The primary manager of a group, with an approved account and an ongoing role independent of who hosts a particular movie night.
_Avoid_: Group host, host (for group management)

**Group admin**:
An additional group manager with an approved account, in an ongoing role independent of who hosts a particular movie night.
_Avoid_: Co-host (for group management)

**Movie night**:
A scheduled group gathering centered on one movie.
_Avoid_: Watch session (when referring to a scheduled gathering)

**Event**:
An individual movie night on the group's calendar, including an occurrence of a recurring series.

**Recurring series**:
A set of movie-night events sharing a repeating schedule.

**Event editor**:
A member authorized to manage an event: its creator, the group owner, or a group admin.

**Scheduling time zone**:
A group's reference time zone for the local meaning of its movie-night schedule.

**RSVP**:
A participant's intended attendance for an event, recorded as Going, Maybe, or Not going; No response means no intention has been recorded.
_Avoid_: Actual attendance

**Actual attendance**:
A record of who joined a movie night, distinguishing full from partial attendance.
_Avoid_: RSVP

**Picker**:
The participant assigned to choose the movie for an event.
_Avoid_: Host (when referring to movie selection)

**Picker rotation**:
A group's process for distributing opportunities to choose movies among its rotation participants.

**Rotation participant**:
A group participant included in the picker rotation, distinct from someone excluded from rotation who may still attend.

**Rotation round**:
One cycle of the picker rotation in which rotation participants each have a regular turn.

**Picker reservation**:
A pending use of a participant's turn associated with an upcoming picker assignment.
_Avoid_: Completed turn, picker credit

**Used turn**:
A turn treated as consumed in the current rotation round, distinct from the participant's lifetime picker total.

**Picker credit**:
Attribution of movie selection to a participant for a completed night.
_Avoid_: Picker reservation

**Lifetime picker total**:
The count of completed movie nights credited to a participant within a particular group.
_Avoid_: Current-round status, assignment count

**Shared watchlist**:
A group's collection of suggested movies for future viewing.
_Avoid_: Personal watchlist

**Interest vote**:
A participant's expression that they would like to watch a suggested movie.
_Avoid_: Rating, movie approval, binding selection vote

**Group history**:
The group's record of completed movie nights and their recorded movie, picker credit, and attendance.
_Avoid_: Personal viewing log
